# Onboarding research: Huntr, Teal and Simplify

How three job-search tools welcome someone new, researched on 2026-10-18.

**Method.** One researcher per tool, working in parallel, using public pages only. Nobody signed up. All three tools hide their first screen behind sign-up, so the findings come from help centers, product pages, store listings and reviews. No public screenshots or videos of a brand-new account's first screen turned up for any of them. Every fact below links to its source. "Not found" means the sources listed didn't say, not that the feature doesn't exist.

## Comparison

| | Huntr | Teal (Job Tracker) | Simplify |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First screen** | Main dashboard, the "central hub" (not documented for a brand-new account) | Job Tracker with a few sample jobs already in it | Profile page with a "Profile Strength" score and a checklist |
| **Asks for** | Account (email or social login); possibly name, username and profile fields, per one older third-party guide | Account; a target date for finding a job, which sets a weekly application goal | Account; target role, location, salary and experience; a matching quiz; resume upload is optional |
| **Time to value** | Once you save your first job, which unlocks the AI tools | Immediately: sample jobs to explore | Job matches "personalized to your profile from the start"; no timeline given |
| **Return trigger** | Weekly and Daily Digest emails | Follow-up date with an emailed reminder on the day; weekly application goal | Daily match emails (on by default); a streak for viewing 5 matches a day; push notifications on iOS |

## Huntr

Sources: Huntr's help center, its App Store listing, Notion pages for career centers, and one third-party guide. The sign-up page, G2, Capterra and the Wayback Machine refused automated access.

**First screen.** After logging in, Huntr opens the main dashboard, which it calls your "central hub." The left navigation has a "Welcome Page," Resume Builder, Resume Reviews, My Job Trackers (with "+" for a new board), AI tools, Autofill and the Chrome Extension ([dashboard][h-a], Sep 2025). The help center describes the dashboard in general, not what a brand-new account sees.

**Asks for.**
- A third-party guide lists email and password or "Sign up with Google," then name and username, email verification, and a profile with job title, industry and bio ([AIPure][h-b], Dec 2024). It contradicts itself about LinkedIn sign-up and may be out of date.
- The App Store version history adds "Social Login" in v2.0.10, 09/04/2024 ([App Store][h-c]).
- The extension guide's steps are install, "Log in to your Huntr account," then save your first job ([extension][h-d], Nov 2025).
- No goals or job-preferences questionnaire for individual users was found.

**Time to value.**
- The AI tools (cover letters, follow-up emails) "become accessible once a job is saved to the Job Tracker" ([dashboard][h-a]).
- Saving with the extension is "the simplest and fastest way to import jobs" ([saving jobs][h-e], May 2025).
- The help center's Quick Start Guide now returns 404 ([quick start][h-f]).

**Sample data, templates, chips, empty state.**
- A new board starts with the stages Wishlist, Applied, Interview, Offer and Rejected, called "just starting points" ([job board][h-g], Apr 2026).
- A "Huntr demo board" is offered to career advisors, with its contents not shown ([onboarding job seekers][h-j]).
- Empty-board wording: not found.
- One App Store review (03/09/2022) said they were "really confused about how to use this without filling up my board with a bunch of test company names" ([App Store][h-c]).

**Brings you back.**
- Account settings offer a Weekly Digest, "A weekly rundown of all your job search data delivered to your inbox," and a Daily Digest ([account settings][h-k], May 2026).
- Activities can be filtered by "urgent, overdue, or completed" ([activities][h-l], Jul 2025), and deadlines "appear directly on your job tile" ([job card][h-m], Oct 2025). No email or push alerts for deadlines are documented.
- Goals ("Apply to 5 jobs per week") and an automatic "Follow Up activity due in 3 days" exist only for career centers ([launch guide][h-o], screenshots from 2021; [onboarding job seekers][h-j]).
- Streaks: not found.

## Teal (Job Tracker)

Sources: Teal's help center and product pages, read as Wayback Machine snapshots because tealhq.com refused automated access, plus Teal's YouTube videos, its Chrome Web Store listing and one competitor's review. Some help articles were last updated a year or more before the snapshot, so the live app may differ.

**First screen.**
- "When you first sign up for Teal, you'll notice a few sample jobs already populated in your Job Tracker!" ([getting started][t-a], snapshot Sep 2025).
- A competitor's review says you "land inside a job tracking dashboard—not a resume editor," and that Teal "immediately encourages you to install its job-saving extension" ([Enhancv][t-b], updated 10/11/2026; a competitor, so possibly biased).
- The Job Tracker menu has a "Quick Tour" ([dashboard][t-d]).

**Asks for.**
- An account, through Gmail, LinkedIn or email, followed by "Setting Up Your Job Search Goals" ([creating an account video][t-e], Oct 2024; only the description and chapter titles were checked).
- A target date: "Upon first signing up… you'll be prompted to select a Target Date for finding a new opportunity." The date sets a weekly application goal, from 12 a week (0–2 months) down to 1 (12+ months), or 5 with no date ([application widget][t-f], snapshot Aug 2025, "Updated over a year ago").
- A resume upload is optional ([Enhancv][t-b]). The Chrome extension is optional, and installing it opens "the onboarding page" ([extension help][t-g]).

**Time to value.**
- The sample jobs are there right away, to "explore… as you get accustomed to the tracking" ([getting started][t-a]).
- Per-stage suggestions appear "After bookmarking a job" ([guidance][t-j]).
- Saving a job by hand opens a detailed view of the role. Pasting a URL doesn't fill in the other fields ([bookmarking][t-i]).

**Sample data, templates, chips, empty state.**
- Sample data: yes, "a few sample jobs" ([getting started][t-a]).
- Templates: "40+ email templates designed for every stage" ([job tracker page][t-k]), which change with the job's stage ([tracker tools][t-l]). There are also per-stage checklists ([guidance][t-j]).
- Empty-state wording and suggestion chips: not found.

**Brings you back.**
- Follow-up date is an editable column ([dashboard][t-d]). For the Applied stage, Teal's guidance is "Follow up… using provided dates/reminders. Send first, second, and third follow-ups," and archive "if no response after 3 weeks" ([guidance][t-j]).
- Email reminders: "you'll get an automated reminder on the day of, right in your inbox" ([Teal blog][t-m], Feb 2025; a blog post, not the help center).
- The weekly goal is tracked Monday to Sunday to "create accountability" ([application widget][t-f]). The store listing says "Set and monitor goals around applications submitted and jobs bookmarked" ([Chrome Web Store][t-n]).
- Streaks, digests and push notifications: not found.

## Simplify

Sources: mainly Simplify's help center, most of it updated around Oct 13, 2026, plus reviews from March to July 2026, two of them by competitors.

**First screen.**
- After sign-up you land on your profile page, which shows a "Profile Strength" score and a checklist ([signing up][s-a]). The score shows "how complete your profile on Simplify is" ([profile strength][s-b]).
- A competitor's review mentions "a welcome screen and onboarding checklist" and "a 6-step onboarding checklist" without describing them ([jobhire.ai][s-c], Jun 2026).
- An extension-first review describes an onboarding that "asks you to upload your resume and build out a profile," with "a progress bar that shows how far along you are" ([HirePilot][s-d], Mar 2026).

**Asks for.**
- Target roles (at least one), where you want to work, salary and experience ([signing up][s-a]).
- A resume upload is optional and called "the fastest way to get started" ([signing up][s-a]). Accepted files are PDF, DOC or DOCX up to 5 MB, and you review the parsed details afterwards ([resume parsing][s-e]).
- A "matching quiz" on roles, places, remote or in person, salary, company size, industries, and what you don't want to see ([match preferences][s-f]).
- After creating an account, "you may see an onboarding prompt to install Copilot," the autofill extension ([Copilot setup][s-g]).

**Time to value.**
- Matches are "already personalized to your profile from the start" and arrive "each day" ([matches vs board][s-h]). No timeline for the first batch is given.
- Autofill needs the extension and a profile: "Copilot uses your Simplify profile as the source for autofill information" ([Copilot setup][s-g]).

**Sample data, templates, chips, empty state.**
- Templates exist, but not in onboarding: AI Email templates grouped by stage ("Send a thank-you, follow up, reschedule, or decline") ([AI email][s-i]).
- Sample data, suggestion chips and empty-state wording: not found.

**Brings you back.**
- Match emails: "Daily notifications are enabled by default," and can be changed to weekly or never ([match preferences][s-f]).
- Streak: "Viewing at least 5 daily matches increases your streak by one day." A 7-day streak makes your profile eligible for recruiters ([dashboard][s-j]). The same article describes an application goal but calls it daily in the text and "weekly" in a caption.
- iOS: "New job recommendations arrive as notifications" ([iOS app][s-k]).
- After you submit an application through Copilot, it prompts you to add it to your tracker ([job tracker][s-l]).
- Follow-up reminders in the tracker: not found. "Follow-ups" appears only as a stage name ([job tracker][s-l]).
