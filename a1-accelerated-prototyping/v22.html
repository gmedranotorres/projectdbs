How I work

  1. Begin with the decisionWho needs this, and what will they do with it?
  2. Examine the data honestlyMissing values, inconsistent definitions, duplicates.
  3. Build a traceable workflowSteps someone else can follow.
  4. Challenge the resultDoes the evidence support it?
  5. Deliver for the audienceA map, a model or a plain explanation.
