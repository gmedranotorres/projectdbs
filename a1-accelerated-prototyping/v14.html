You’re deciding where outreach isn’t reaching people.

That’s what I mapped for COFI.

The problem.An organizing team’s field records sat in spreadsheets, with community names spelled a dozen ways.

What I built.Standardized names, matched records to Chicago community areas, and published a live choropleth of doors knocked, conversations, flyers and referrals.

Why it lasts.Updates flow automatically from Google Sheets, and the system is documented in both languages the team works in.

Dosidicus gigas, the jumbo squid.

The thesis.At Universidad Nacional Agraria La Molina I studied how a clay-derived additive changes texture, gel strength and water-holding capacity in squid surimi gels.

The question behind it.How can an abundant marine resource from the Humboldt Current become a higher-value product?
