Description

A bilingual analyst of considerable range. Distinguished from related species by the habit of cleaning a dataset before modelling it, and of documenting her work in English and Spanish so that others may maintain it.

Habitat and range

First recorded at La Molina, Lima (12° S), completing a degree in Fisheries Engineering, ranked fourth of its class. Observed at Orihuela, Spain (38° N) in 2019, in food-science laboratories. Long resident in the business-intelligence waters of Peru, 2021 to 2025. Now established at Chicago (41° N), at the University of Chicago, where it is expected to graduate in June 2027.

Habits

Builds dashboards, upward of thirty recorded. Maps neighbourhood outreach, merges public datasets, and teaches programming to Spanish-speaking beginners. Migrates vertically, like the squid it once studied: by day in the depths of raw records, by night rising to the surface with decisions.
