### Mapping outreach across Chicago neighborhoods

During my work with [Community Organizing and Family Issues (COFI)](https://cofionline.org/), I created a neighborhood-level dashboard for community outreach data: [COFI Community Outreach Dashboard](https://gmedranotorres.github.io/cofi-dashboard/)

The workflow connected Google Sheets, Google Apps Script, Leaflet, and GitHub Pages. It transformed field records into an interactive map and a set of indicators covering doors knocked, conversations with residents, flyers distributed, and referrals.

Key contributions:

- Automated the movement of updated outreach data into the dashboard
- Standardized inconsistent community names before visualization
- Designed an interactive choropleth map for comparing activity across neighborhoods
- Created summary indicators that staff could understand at a glance
- Documented the system so it could be maintained after the internship

The value of this project was not simply the map. It gave staff a clearer way to see where outreach was happening and where additional attention might be needed.

### MortgageMatch: exploring mortgage lending in Illinois

For a Data for Decisions team project at UChicago, I helped develop [MortgageMatch](https://github.com/uchicago-2026-capp30122/project-data-4-decisions), an interactive dashboard for examining mortgage approval and denial patterns across Illinois.

The project brings together three public data sources: application-level records from the Home Mortgage Disclosure Act (HMDA), county-level indicators from the U.S. Census American Community Survey, and HMDA lender reference data. Our workflow downloads and cleans the raw files, standardizes county and lender identifiers, constructs analysis-ready variables—including approval outcomes, income ranges, and loan amount bands—and merges the sources into a unified dataset.

Through the dashboard, users can filter applicants by characteristics such as age and income, then compare lending outcomes across counties, institutions, income groups, loan purposes, and loan amounts.

MortgageMatch demonstrates how complex public records can be transformed into an accessible tool for investigating differences in mortgage lending across borrowers, places, and financial institutions.

### Exploring sustainable uses for giant squid

My undergraduate research examined a clay-derived additive containing nanoscale crystalline domains and its relationship to the texture and stability of gel products made from giant squid (*Dosidicus gigas*) surimi.

The project brought together experimental design, laboratory measurements, scientific interpretation, and food-product development. It also asked a broader sustainability question: how can an abundant marine resource be transformed into a higher-value product?

Through this research and my involvement with the DeFoPro-Gel research community, I became especially interested in work that connects scientific evidence, resource management, and food-system innovation.

## How I approach a project

1. **Begin with the decision.** I first clarify who needs the analysis and what they should be able to learn or do with it.
2. **Examine the data honestly.** I look for missing values, inconsistent definitions, duplicates, and limitations before modeling.
3. **Build a traceable workflow.** Transformations and assumptions should be organized well enough for someone else to follow.
4. **Challenge the result.** I validate outputs and consider whether the interpretation is supported by the evidence.
5. **Deliver for the audience.** The final form might be a model, notebook, map, dashboard, or written explanation—but it should always be accessible to its users.
