# AGENTS.md: Gabriela's landing page (MPCS 51238, Assignment 1)

## The idea
A landing page for Gabriela Medrano Torres: fisheries engineer, former business intelligence analyst, M.S. student in Computational Analysis and Public Policy at UChicago (graduating June 2027).

## Who it's for
Hiring managers and research leads in Chicago working on policy research, civic tech, environmental data, economic consulting or applied analytics; also community partners and faculty.

## What a visitor should understand
She turns messy, real-world records into tools people use to decide. Proof: COFI outreach dashboard, MortgageMatch (team project), 30+ Power BI dashboards, jumbo squid surimi research. Bilingual (English/Spanish). Peru, Spain, Chicago.

## What a visitor should do
Open a project, then get in touch (GitHub for now).

## Assignment goals
- 25 versions, v01.html to v25.html: go wide (v01–v15), combine (v16–v20), converge (v21–v25). v25 is the final.
- index.html is the gallery: every version with a thumbnail, a link and a short note on what changed.
- A new direction must change layout, structure, mood or audience; colors or fonts alone don't count.
- At least 20 designs must look unlike anything else in the class: build from Gabriela's own artifacts (khipu, squid, nautical chart, CTA map, COFI canvassing), not generic styles.

## Constraints
- Plain HTML and CSS, optional vanilla JS. No frameworks, packages, build step, external APIs or data storage (no localStorage). Push back if a tool suggests one.
- Fonts are self-hosted in assets/fonts (fonts.css). Never link to Google Fonts or other CDNs.
- Every version is a frozen snapshot: never edit an older version to make a new one. Copy it to the next number.
- Every version links back to the gallery (index.html) and shows its version number.
- Thumbnails live in assets/thumbs/vNN.webp (1440x900 screenshot scaled to 640x400).
- Deploy: GitHub repo, then Vercel Hobby with the "Other" preset and no build command. vercel.json only turns on clean URLs.

- Nothing personal beyond what is already public in READMEGitHub.md and her résumé.
