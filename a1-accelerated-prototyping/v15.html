480 mWhere squid spend the day

Dosidicus gigas

My thesis at Universidad Nacional Agraria La Molina studied how a clay-derived additive changes texture, gel strength and water-holding capacity in jumbo squid surimi gels: a way to turn an abundant Humboldt Current resource into a higher-value food.

The jumbo squid spends its days in the deep and rises to the surface at night. Good analysis makes the same trip, from raw records up to a decision.
